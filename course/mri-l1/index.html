MRI Service — Level 1
Safety, cryogenics and preventive maintenance

A healthy MRI magnet loses almost no helium: the cold head turns the boil-off back into liquid. When the cold head, the compressor or the chiller stops, the level starts to fall — and nobody notices until it is expensive. This course teaches the engineer who services the scanner to see it coming.

Why this course

  • Manufacturer service training for a single modality can cost more than ten thousand dollars per course, plus travel.
  • Most engineers learn MRI from forum threads and from the colleague who happened to be there last time.
  • The costly mistakes are not exotic: a refill with no diagnosis, a chiller nobody checked, a door contact that ruins images, a tool carried into Zone IV.

After the course you can

Work safely in the magnet zone and keep others safe: zones, projectiles, tools, what to do if someone is pinned.
Read helium level and pressure trends and tell a real loss from sensor noise; know what to check before anyone orders a refill.
Explain a quench, inspect the quench pipe and know which button is which.
Inspect the RF cage, chiller, power and climate, and catch the infrastructure faults that look like electronics.
Run a structured PM visit by checklist and run and trend the phantom QA.
Write a PM report that protects you and the customer: zero measurement, control points, out-of-scope, do-not-use.

FAQ

Is this manufacturer training?

No. It is independent, vendor-neutral training written by field engineers. It does not replace the manufacturer's procedures, and it does not grant manufacturer authorisation or access to service software.

What does the certificate say?

Your name, the course, the hours, the date and your exam score, with a unique number and a public verification page. It is a certificate of completion of training issued by Service Engineer Academy.

What do I need?

Basic electrical and technical knowledge. No MRI experience is required for Level 1.

About four hours of lessons plus a 45-minute exam. You keep access to the course.
