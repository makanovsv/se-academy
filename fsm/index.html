The records your hospital customers are audited on.

Healthcare regulators expect a documented service history and planned maintenance for each medical device. Dubai's standard is a clear example.

"All service records, test results, calibration and adjustment and QA/QC records should be documented and maintained for each piece of equipment."

Dubai Health Authority, Standards for Medical Equipment Management (DHA/HRS/HPSD/ST-38), in force since January 2024

When your team works in Service Engineer, that history is produced as a by-product of the job. Your customer can open it at any time.
