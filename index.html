Equipment

how it works

MRI

A superconducting magnet, three gradient coils and an RF chain that turn hydrogen nuclei into a spatially encoded radio signal, then into images.
how it works

CT

An X-ray tube and a curved multi-row detector rotating around the patient on a slip-ring gantry, measuring attenuation from hundreds of angles and reconstructing cross-sectional images.
how it works

X-ray / DR

A high-voltage generator drives an X-ray tube for a short exposure; a flat-panel detector turns the transmitted radiation into a digital projection image.
1 model page

Ultrasound

A probe of piezoelectric elements sends short acoustic pulses into tissue and listens for echoes; the system beamforms them into real-time images and Doppler flow data.
2 model pages

ICU ventilator

A closed-loop gas delivery system that mixes air and oxygen, delivers controlled breaths through valves and sensors, and protects the patient with alarms and a safety valve.
1 model page

Anaesthesia workstation

A gas delivery system, vaporiser and rebreathing circle with a ventilator, built so that the patient always receives oxygen and the anaesthetist always knows what is being delivered.
how it works

Patient monitor

A set of sensor front ends (ECG, SpO2, NIBP, IBP, temperature, CO2) feeding one processor that displays trends and raises alarms, often over a hospital network.
1 model page

Clinical chemistry analyzer

An automated photometer: it pipettes samples and reagents into cuvettes, incubates them at a controlled temperature, and measures colour change or ion potentials to calculate analyte concentrations.

Avance family (Datex-Ohmeda S/5 Avance, Avance CS2)

Datex-Ohmeda / GE anaesthesia workstations with an electronic gas mixer, an integrated bellows ventilator and a compact breathing system, used in operating theatres for general anaesthesia from adults to children.
ICU ventilator · GE HealthCare

Carescape R860

Pneumatically driven ICU ventilator for adult, paediatric and neonatal patients that runs from wall (or compressor) air and oxygen and is used in intensive care units and step-down wards.
Ultrasound · GE HealthCare

Voluson E6 / E8 / E10

Premium cart-based ultrasound system built around 3D/4D volume imaging, used mainly in obstetrics, gynaecology and fetal medicine departments.
Clinical chemistry analyzer · Mindray

BS-240

Benchtop, fully automated discrete clinical chemistry analyzer with an optional ISE module, used in small and mid-size hospital and private laboratories for routine biochemistry.
ICU ventilator · Mindray

SV600 / SV650

Mindray's mid-to-high-end ICU ventilator for adult, paediatric and (with the neonatal module) neonatal patients, running on wall air and O2 or on an optional internal blower, used in ICUs, emergency departments and for in-hospital transport.
